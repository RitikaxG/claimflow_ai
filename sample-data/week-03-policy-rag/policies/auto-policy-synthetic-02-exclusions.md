# Auto Policy Synthetic 02 — Exclusions

POLICY_ID: AUTO-SYNTH-002
PRODUCT_TYPE: auto_insurance
VERSION: v1
SOURCE_TYPE: SYNTHETIC

## CLAUSE EX-LIC-001: Invalid license exclusion

The policy does not cover a claim if the person driving the insured vehicle did not hold a valid driving license at the time of the incident.

## CLAUSE EX-ALC-001: Intoxication exclusion

The policy does not cover a claim if the driver was under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or intoxicating substances at the time of the incident.

## CLAUSE EX-COM-001: Commercial use exclusion

The policy does not cover damage when a private vehicle is used for commercial delivery, paid passenger transport, rental service, or other commercial purpose unless commercial use is specifically covered by endorsement.

## CLAUSE EX-WEAR-001: Wear and tear exclusion

The policy does not cover normal wear and tear, gradual deterioration, depreciation, mechanical breakdown, or damage caused only by lack of maintenance.
