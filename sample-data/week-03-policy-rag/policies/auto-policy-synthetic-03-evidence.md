# Auto Policy Synthetic 03 — Required Evidence

POLICY_ID: AUTO-SYNTH-003
PRODUCT_TYPE: auto_insurance
VERSION: v1
SOURCE_TYPE: SYNTHETIC

## CLAUSE EV-TH-001: Theft claim evidence requirements

Theft claims require a police FIR number or police report evidence, police station details, claim form, policy number, vehicle registration number, and theft incident description.

## CLAUSE EV-TP-001: Third-party claim evidence requirements

Third-party claims require police report evidence, incident location, third-party details if available, claim form, and description of third-party damage or injury.

## CLAUSE EV-OD-001: Own damage evidence requirements

Own damage claims require claim form, policy number, vehicle registration number, incident description, damage photos or inspection evidence, and repair estimate if repair cost is claimed.

## CLAUSE EV-FLD-001: Flood damage evidence requirements

Flood damage claims require incident location, incident date, flood or waterlogging evidence, damage photos or inspection evidence, and confirmation that no exclusion applies.
