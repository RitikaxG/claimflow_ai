# Auto Policy Synthetic 01 — Base Coverages

POLICY_ID: AUTO-SYNTH-001
PRODUCT_TYPE: auto_insurance
VERSION: v1
SOURCE_TYPE: SYNTHETIC

## CLAUSE COV-OD-001: Own damage coverage

The policy covers accidental physical damage to the insured private vehicle caused by collision, overturning, fire, external impact, flood, storm, or malicious damage, subject to policy terms, deductible, exclusions, and required evidence.

## CLAUSE COV-TP-001: Third-party liability coverage

The policy covers legal liability for bodily injury, death, or property damage caused to a third party by the insured vehicle, subject to valid incident documentation and required police or legal evidence where applicable.

## CLAUSE COV-TH-001: Theft coverage

The policy covers theft of the insured private vehicle when the theft is reported to police and the required theft claim documents are submitted.

## CLAUSE LIMIT-RP-001: Repair estimate approval limit

Repair estimates above INR 75,000 require insurer review before final approval. A repair estimate alone is not sufficient for claim approval unless policy coverage and required evidence are also satisfied.
