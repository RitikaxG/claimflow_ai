Claims work rarely fails because teams lack data. It slows down because the story is scattered. ClaimFlow brings that story into one clear, reviewable journey.

A reviewer creates an account, signs in, and starts a claim from the material already received: one PDF, or the original email.

ClaimFlow extracts the facts, organizes the claim, and validates completeness. Missing fields and required evidence become visible immediately, without exposing raw extraction details to the reviewer.

When policy context is needed, the reviewer can ask a claim-specific coverage question. Retrieval grounds the answer in relevant policy evidence and keeps the source available for review.

When the next step is unclear, the agent recommends it. Every tool call is guarded, recorded, and kept behind human approval. A request for missing information can be prepared, returned to the reviewer, matched to the submitted source, and added to the claim.

Similar completed reviews provide workflow memory—not claim evidence. Reviewers can rate that guidance, improving future retrieval.

The reviewer can edit the prepared facts and approve, while the final decision always stays human.

From intake through validation, retrieval, agent action, memory, review, and resolution, ClaimFlow preserves the full trace. Operations teams can inspect the pipeline and evaluation reports for grounding, safety, and quality.
